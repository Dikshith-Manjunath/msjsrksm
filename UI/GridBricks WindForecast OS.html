What it does

Built for decisions made in minutes.

Every figure is chosen to be read in a glance, and every time frame is labelled, so nobody has to guess which forecast they are looking at.

01

Sub-second scannability

The imbalance figures, MW swings and gate-closure countdown are the largest things on the page.

02

Temporal anchoring

Every view names its time frame: delivery day, decision day and the gate-closure cutoff.

03

Gate-closure playback

Step the cutoff from 06:00 to 15:00 and watch the forecast change as newer weather runs arrive.

Two views

One for the desk, one for the review.

Use the Decision Desk to bid a single day, and Risk & Evaluation to check accuracy and bias across a month.

Operational · daily

Decision Desk

  • Pick a delivery day and gate-closure cutoff.
  • Actual against forecast, with the error for each period.
  • Hedging notes for the evening peak.
  • Export the day as CSV or JSON.

Analytical · monthly

Risk & Evaluation

  • Scorecard: WAPE, R², bias ratio and net GWh.
  • Error distribution with a normal fit and tail check.
  • Hourly error box plot to find weak hours.
  • Filter by week, peak or off-peak hours.

Method

The measures behind the numbers.

Each figure says something different about the forecast. Read them together, not one at a time.

MAE · RMSE

Size of the miss

Average error, and a heavier penalty on large swings.

Bias

Direction of the miss

Forecast above or below actual, and how often.

WAPE · R²

Share and fit

Error as a share of generation, and how closely the forecast tracks it.

Net GWh

Energy at stake

The net volume of energy the forecast got wrong.
